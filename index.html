Methodology and interpretation

Results describe the currently filtered records. Numeric statistics exclude values that cannot be parsed as numbers. Variance and standard deviation use population formulas. Outliers use the 1.5 × IQR screening rule.

Correlation is calculated on paired numeric values and does not imply causation. Group comparisons depend on the chosen measure and aggregation. Verify units, missing data, duplicates, identifiers and geographic coverage before official use.
